# Principles

1. Integrate, do not replace by default.
2. Stable semantics, replaceable implementations.
3. Capabilities over product SKUs.
4. Intent over actuation.
5. Vendor complexity stays below the adapter boundary.
6. Simulation is a first-class runtime.
7. Deterministic scenarios should be replayable.
8. Loss of the platform must allow validated local control to remain/fall back safe.
9. Simple by default, deep when requested.
10. Openness does not mean rewriting mature libraries/standards.
11. Avoid all-or-nothing adoption.
12. No real passenger-elevator control during early milestones.
